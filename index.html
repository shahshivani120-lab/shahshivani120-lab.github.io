Feature 01 — Organic search + AI visibilitySRA HQ · USA · May 2024 – Sep 2026

The firm Copilot kept quoting.

Survey Research Associates is a 30-year-old US firm serving AmLaw-tier law firms. It had almost no search visibility. Its pages became the source AI assistants cited for buyer questions in its category, often ahead of the software vendors' own sites.

The problem

Law-firm leaders were researching retention, performance reviews and legal AI tools, and SRA wasn't in those answers on Google or in AI assistants.

What I did

  • Rebuilt website content, technical and on-page SEO in Webflow
  • Mapped buyer questions into comparison, buyer-guide and research content
  • Tracked AI citations in Bing Webmaster Tools and doubled down on the formats AI kept quoting

Clicks grew 166% while impressions grew 19%. The right pages, not just more pages.

Feature 02 — Client marketing, start to finishRestoGenius · Australia · Nov 2025 – Present

Seven restaurants, one system.

RestoGenius is an Australian restaurant POS SaaS that also runs marketing for its restaurant customers. I lead that work and RestoGenius's own marketing, with a team of 3.

Websites & SEO

Three new restaurant websites earned 1,647 Google clicks in their first 3 months. The two largest ranked at an average position of 5.9. Across the domain, average position improved from 17.5 to 8.2.

Meta Ads · creative over budget

79 WhatsApp and Messenger contacts from ~US$1,050 (~US$13 each). Costs ranged about 10x across 19 campaigns, and the expensive ones had weaker hooks and offers, not bigger budgets.

Organic visitors enquired at nearly 4x the rate of direct, but only 8% reached pricing. So that's what we fixed next.

Feature 03 — LinkedIn ghostwriting & personal brandFounder, US-based B2B IT company · 17–23 Nov 2026

One week, 73,322 impressions.

I ghostwrite founder-led LinkedIn content for B2B founders, turning their expertise, opinions and stories into posts that sound like them and reach the buyers they want. These results are from the founder of a US-based B2B IT company (client anonymised).

The goal

Grow an IT company founder's reach with buyers and decision-makers beyond an existing following of ~17K, and turn that attention into profile visits, conversations and clicks.

What I did

  • Captured the founder's voice, opinions and stories through short interviews
  • Wrote posts around buyer problems, not product features, with strong hooks
  • Built the profile and call-to-action so attention turned into visits and clicks

A comment for every two reactions: people weren't just scrolling past, they were talking back.
