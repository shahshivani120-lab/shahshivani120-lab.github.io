Case study 01

SRA HQ | Organic search + AI visibility

Survey Research Associates is a 30-year-old US firm serving AmLaw-tier law firms. I led its digital marketing remotely from May 2024 to September 2026.

190KGoogle impressions over 16 months
+166%Google clicks, Q3 vs Q2 2026 (273 → 725)
2.8KAI citations in September 2026
86%AI citation share, “best performance management software for law firms”
The problem

A respected firm with almost no search visibility. Law-firm leaders were researching retention, performance reviews and legal AI tools, and SRA wasn't in those answers on Google or in AI assistants.

What I did
  • Rebuilt website content, technical and on-page SEO in Webflow
  • Mapped buyer questions and wrote comparison, buyer-guide and research content
  • Tracked AI citations in Bing Webmaster Tools and doubled down on the formats AI kept quoting
The result

SRA's pages became the source Microsoft Copilot quoted for buyer questions in its category, often ahead of the software vendors' own sites. Clicks grew far faster than impressions.

Websites & SEO

Three new restaurant websites earned 1,647 Google clicks in their first 3 months. The two largest ranked at an average position of 5.9, with CTRs of 4.7% and 9.9%. Across the domain, average position improved from 17.5 to 8.2.

In GA4, organic visitors enquired at 2.12%, nearly 4x the 0.55% from direct, but only ~8% reached pricing. So I prioritised CTA hierarchy, navigation to pricing and demo, cleaner UTMs and qualified-lead tracking.

Meta Ads · creative over budget

RestoGenius's own WhatsApp and Messenger campaigns in 2026: 79 contacts from ~US$1,050 (~US$13 each). Costs ranged about 10x across 19 campaigns, and the most expensive ones had weaker hooks and offers, not bigger budgets.

Testing pain-point hooks and clear offers before scaling brought the best campaigns to ~₹515 per conversation. The Brisbane Kiosk + POS launch got 7 conversations below the account average.
